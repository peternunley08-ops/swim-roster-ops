The poolside reality

Clipboards. Group texts. A whiteboard that's always wrong.

Swim programs run on paper rosters, a spreadsheet someone forgot to update, and a flurry of "who's covering Level 3?" texts. The big registration platforms handle sign-ups and billing — but nobody built the simple thing your staff need on the deck, in the moment. So we did.

What DeckHand does

Everything that happens at the pool today

One clean app for the daily operations the registration systems ignore.

📋

Rosters & attendance

Every class, every swimmer, attendance taken with a tap — on any phone. Add, move, and find swimmers instantly. Print or export when you need paper.

The part nobody else does
🗻

Lifeguard rotation & staff coverage

Auto or manual rotation across stations, who's on duty today, and one-tap no-show → find a replacement from whoever's actually here and qualified.

⚡

Live "right now"

Who's teaching and guarding this minute, understaffed-class alerts, and attendance at a glance — so the deck manager always knows where the gaps are.

👥

Staff & roles

Instructors, lifeguards, or both — one shared people list. Daily check-in that resets each morning. Rename someone once; it updates everywhere.

🔁

Trades & requests

Instructors request a swap, pick-up, or drop from their phone; you approve with a tap and it applies to the schedule instantly.

📱

Built for the deck

Installs like an app, works with no signal, syncs across every device, and is readable in bright sun. Real logins, real security.
